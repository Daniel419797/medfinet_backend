# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Medfinet community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, experiences and cultural contexts;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologising and learning from mistakes;
- prioritising the community and the safety of the people Medfinet is intended to serve.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- sexualised language, imagery or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sharing identifiable health information, credentials or private security reports;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces, events or communications.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **danieladedayooluwole@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible. Do not use public issues to report harassment, personal data exposure or security incidents.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, impact and pattern of behaviour. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction or permanent ban. Retaliation against reporters or participants in an investigation is prohibited.
